Your First Cruise, Made Easy

Pearla and Aaron, this guide is designed to take the guesswork out of your very first cruise. You are starting with a small-ship river journey, which means a relaxed pace, intimate ports, spectacular scenery and far fewer of the crowds associated with large ocean ships.

Your trip begins with flights from Houston to Portland, an overnight near the airport, a cruise-line-arranged transfer to American West, five days of river cruising, and a cruise-line-managed transfer back to Portland International Airport for your flights home.

Before You Leave Home

For a first cruise, the easiest trip is the one where you know what goes in which bag, what happens to your luggage, and what you will actually wear. This section is your practical checklist.

The Simple Luggage Plan

Best setup for each of you: one checked suitcase plus one small carry-on or personal item. American Cruise Lines does not impose a cruise luggage limit, but it recommends bringing only what you need. Suitcases under about 10 inches high can usually slide under the bed once unpacked.

For your Southwest flights, one carry-on plus one personal item is free. For U.S. mainland travel ticketed or changed on or after April 9, 2026, Southwest's standard first checked bag fee is $45 and the second is $55 for Basic, Choice and Choice Preferred fares unless your fare, status or credit-card benefit includes checked bags.

Checked Suitcase

  • 5–6 casual daytime tops
  • 3–4 pairs of pants or travel bottoms
  • 1–2 nicer casual dinner outfits
  • Underwear, socks and sleepwear
  • Light sweater or fleece
  • Waterproof hooded rain jacket
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Optional second pair of shoes for dinner
  • Toiletries larger than TSA carry-on limits
  • Small foldable tote or day bag

Carry-On / Personal Item

  • Government-issued photo ID
  • Prescription medications
  • Phone, charging cable and portable charger
  • Glasses, hearing aids and other daily essentials
  • Wallet, credit cards and a small amount of cash
  • Travel documents and confirmation information
  • One change of clothes
  • Rain shell or light layer
  • Anything you would be upset to lose in checked luggage

Complete Columbia River Packing List

Dress code without the mystery: American Cruise Lines recommends casual resort-style clothing onboard and ashore. Dinner is still relaxed: think nice slacks and a collared shirt or sweater for Aaron, and a casual dress or nice top with slacks for Pearla. There are no formal nights to pack for.
Helpful first-cruise hint: Download the Faye app before leaving home and make sure your policy appears in the app. It gives you quick access to 24/7 assistance, travel documents, flight alerts and claims support.

How Your Luggage Will Work

Airport → Hotel → Ship → Airport

On Flight Day

Tag checked bags with your name and mobile number, but keep identification, medicine, valuables, electronics and a change of clothes with you. Southwest's standard checked-bag size limit is 62 linear inches and 50 pounds per bag. You can also track checked bags digitally in the Southwest app.

At the Portland Hotel

Because the pre-cruise hotel stay is part of your American Cruise Lines program, the cruise line includes luggage service and transportation from the hotel to the ship. Follow the cruise-line instructions at the hotel for when and where to have luggage ready the morning of embarkation.

At Embarkation

You do not have to drag your large suitcase around the ship. American Cruise Lines crew members take your tagged luggage and deliver it to your stateroom. Keep your carry-on with you until you are settled in your cabin.

Once in Stateroom 226

Unpack fully, then store the empty suitcase under the bed if it fits. American Cruise Lines specifically recommends luggage under about 10 inches high for easy under-bed storage.

On the Last Night

Follow the ship's final luggage instructions carefully. Pack everything you will not need in the morning, but keep your ID, medications, phone, charger, travel documents and your next-day clothing accessible. Do not accidentally pack the shoes you need to walk off the ship. Yes, people do that. 😊

Embarkation Day: What Actually Happens

Saturday, October 24

1. Breakfast and Cruise-Line Instructions

Enjoy breakfast at the hotel and follow the meeting time provided by American Cruise Lines. Your hotel-to-ship transfer is included, so there is no need to arrange your own ride.

2. Have Your Luggage Ready

Attach the cruise luggage tags provided for the sailing and remove any old cruise luggage tags. The cruise line handles luggage service from the hotel program and crew members bring your larger bags to your stateroom.

3. Boarding the Ship

American Cruise Lines' normal boarding window is approximately 11:30 AM–12:30 PM, with departure generally at 2:00 PM or earlier once all guests have arrived. Because you are using their hotel-and-transfer program, follow the transfer schedule they provide rather than worrying about arriving independently.

4. Keep a Small Bag With You

Your ID, medicine, phone, glasses, valuables and anything you might want before your suitcase reaches the room should stay in your carry-on.

5. Find Stateroom 226 and Settle In

Your Deluxe Balcony 2 stateroom is about 236 square feet with floor-to-ceiling sliding doors and a private balcony. Once the bags arrive, unpack and put the suitcase away. River cruising is wonderfully simple because you unpack once.

6. Learn the Ship Before You Need To

Locate the restaurant, River Lounge, Paddlewheel Lounge, Sun Deck, elevator and gangway. Knowing your way around on day one makes the rest of the cruise effortless.

7. Attend Any Welcome or Safety Briefing

Pay attention even if it feels routine. You will learn the ship's emergency procedures plus useful information about meal times, excursions and the following day's schedule.

First-cruise rule: Do not spend sail-away unpacking socks. Step outside, take in the river, and enjoy the fact that your first cruise has officially begun.

At Hobby Airport

For a 5:55 AM departure, plan to be inside the terminal roughly two hours before your flight. Go through security, locate the gate first, then grab coffee or breakfast.

During the Las Vegas Connection

Once you land, check the Southwest app or airport monitors for the Portland gate and head there first. Your checked luggage should continue to Portland on a through reservation, so there is no need to retrieve it in Las Vegas.

Arriving in Portland

After landing at PDX at 11:30 AM, follow signs to baggage claim. Your overnight stay is at the Embassy Suites by Hilton Portland Airport, with hotel transfer service noted in your itinerary. Check-in begins at 3:00 PM.

Lisa's suggestion: Keep the arrival afternoon easy. This is not the day to race around Portland checking off sights. Have lunch, settle in and get a good night's sleep so your first cruise begins relaxed.

Sights & Landmarks

These are the places that give the Columbia River Gorge its sense of scale, history and character.

MUST DOSCENIC

Multnomah Falls

At 620 feet, Multnomah Falls is the Gorge's signature waterfall. The dramatic two-tiered drop, moss-covered cliffs and Benson Bridge create one of the Pacific Northwest's most recognizable scenes.

Time NeededAbout 60–90 minutes at the falls
CrowdsPopular; mornings are better
Best TimeYour scheduled morning visit
SurpriseYou get a huge view without a strenuous hike

Your excursion: Sunday, October 25 at 9:00 AM, approximately 2.6 hours including transportation and time at the falls.

Sunset & Special Moments

MUST DO

Golden Hour on the Sun Deck

Late afternoon can turn the Gorge's basalt walls warm gold while the river reflects the sky. Take a jacket, bring a drink and give yourselves ten quiet minutes with the phones put away.

MUST DOUNIQUE TO RIVER CRUISING

Watch a Lock Transit

If the ship enters a lock while you are awake, head outside. The vessel enters what feels like a giant concrete room, the gates close and the water level slowly rises or falls before the opposite gates open. Ask the crew when a lock transit is expected because navigation timing can shift.

HIDDEN GEM

A Rainy Gorge Morning

Do not assume gray weather means a disappointing day. Low clouds wrapping around forested cliffs can make the Gorge feel almost cinematic, and waterfalls can look even better after rain. A hooded waterproof jacket is more useful than an umbrella when the wind picks up.

What's Overhyped?

OVERRATED — AT PEAK CROWD TIME

Multnomah Falls in the Middle of a Busy Day

The waterfall itself is absolutely worth seeing. What can be overrated is battling peak crowds for the same view. Your scheduled morning excursion is the right approach: enjoy it, get the photos, walk toward Benson Bridge and then keep exploring the Gorge.

OVERRATED

Trying to “See Portland” on Arrival Day

You will have started before dawn in Houston. A packed Portland sightseeing afternoon sounds efficient but usually just makes the first day exhausting. Rest, have a good meal and begin the cruise fresh.

First-Cruise Secrets Nobody Tells You

  • Read the daily program every evening. Excursion meeting times and onboard activities can change.
  • Take a photo of the daily schedule. Then you always have it with you.
  • Use your balcony. On this itinerary, the scenery is part of what you paid for.
  • You do not have to do everything. Missing an activity because you are enjoying the river is still cruising correctly.
  • Eat with other guests at least once. Small-ship river cruising is wonderfully social.
  • Ask the crew questions. They know when scenery, locks and important river moments are coming.
  • Use airplane mode onboard. Connect to the ship's Wi-Fi rather than letting your phone roam unnecessarily.

Thursday, October 29 • Disembarkation & Heading Home

American West → PDX → Dallas Love Field → Houston

The Night Before

Read the ship's final disembarkation notice before going to bed. It will tell you when luggage should be ready, when breakfast is served and where to meet for your airport transfer. Pack almost everything, but keep out your morning toiletries, medications, ID, phone, charger, travel documents and the clothes and shoes you will wear off the ship.

Morning of Disembarkation

American Cruise Lines normally serves breakfast on the final morning. On most sailings, guests may remain onboard until about 8:30 AM. Your exact timing will be set onboard, and the cruise line is handling your transfer to PDX.

Your Luggage

Follow the crew's luggage instructions rather than carrying large suitcases off yourselves. Before leaving the ship, visually confirm that you have your correct bags and make a quick sweep of the stateroom: closet, drawers, bathroom, outlets, nightstands and under the bed.

Before You Step Off the Ship

  • ID and wallet physically in hand or personal bag
  • Phone and charger accounted for
  • All medications packed in your carry-on
  • Nothing left in the room safe, drawers or bathroom
  • Carry-on stays with you for the airport transfer

Airport Transfer

Your ship-to-airport transportation is included and coordinated by American Cruise Lines. The ship's arrival time is approximate; you do not need to manage that timing yourself. Follow the onboard transfer schedule provided by the Excursions Director and crew.

Lisa's Five Don't-Miss Moments for Your First Cruise

  1. Multnomah Falls — one of the Pacific Northwest's signature sights.
  2. The e-bike adventure — if you are both comfortable cyclists.
  3. Columbia Gorge Discovery Center — the best context for understanding what you are seeing.
  4. Maryhill Winery — scenery, vineyard history and wine in one relaxed afternoon.
