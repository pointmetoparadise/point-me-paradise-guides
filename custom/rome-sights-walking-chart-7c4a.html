  • Where the numbers come from. Walks to Trevi, the Spanish Steps, the Pantheon, Piazza Navona and Piazza del Popolo match the hotel guides where the guides give them. The rest are estimates worked out from each hotel's position on the map. Allow a few minutes either side.
  • Hills. The Via Veneto hotels, the EDITION and Gran Filippo sit above Piazza Barberini. Walks leaving them run downhill and the return is a climb.
  • Spanish Steps. From Barberini and Via Veneto you arrive at the top of the staircase. From Mario de' Fiori 37, H10 and Albergo Etico you arrive at the foot.
  • Vatican. St. Peter's is a taxi from every hotel except the Indigo St. George, which is about 18 minutes away on foot. The Vatican Museums entrance is a taxi from all thirteen.
