Dubrovnik

The Walled City, Islands & the Dalmatian Coast
Cruise Day 7 · Enchanted Princess

Dubrovnik is the walled city on Croatia's far southern coast, with marble streets, terracotta roofs and the Adriatic on three sides. The coast north of the city runs past vineyards, oyster farms and islands, including Korčula, often called a smaller Dubrovnik.

Choosing Your Dubrovnik Day

Dubrovnik's Old Town is beautiful but full of stairs. Almost every side street is a staircase, and the city walls and fortress are steep climbs. The easiest way to enjoy it is from above, and then out along the coast and countryside.

Want the classic views, with lunch?

Options B and C drive up Mount Srđ for the famous view of the walled city, then head to seaside Cavtat. Option B, which Peggy liked, ends with a long family lunch in the countryside.

Want a full day out?

Option A, Peggy's pick, goes up the coast to the island of Korčula, with wine, oysters and a stroll through its old town.

Want something short and fun?

Option E is a quick ride in a vintage car up to the Mount Srđ lookout.

Interested in Game of Thrones?

Lisa mentioned an interest in this one. Option F is a guided walk along the flat main street to the famous filming spots, and anyone can join her while the others relax at a café. Option G is Kensington's fuller Game of Thrones day, with the walls, the fortress and Lokrum Island, for anyone up for more climbing.

If the group splits for the day, that's perfectly OK. Everyone meets back on the ship.

Good to Know

The Old Town

The main street, the Stradun, is flat but paved with polished limestone that can be slippery, so good shoes are a must. Most side streets are staircases, and the city walls and fortress are steep climbs, so none of our tours include them.

The easy way to see it

Mount Srđ, reached by road, has the classic view over the walled city with almost no walking.

Cavtat

A small town about 20 minutes south, with a flat waterfront and cafés. It's a much gentler outing than the Old Town.
