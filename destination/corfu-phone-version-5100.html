Corfu

Paleokastritsa, Old Town & the Island's Coast
Cruise Day 5 · Enchanted Princess

Corfu is a green, hilly island off Greece's northwest coast. Its Old Town is a mix of Venetian, French and British influence, and on the far side of the island, Paleokastritsa is known for its clear bays, sea caves and a monastery set above the water.

Every tour below is private, just for our group, with its own driver. The cruise pier sits outside Old Town, so each tour includes pickup at the ship.

How to read the pace labels

Pace: Seated & ScenicMostly riding or sitting, with short, easy stops
Pace: Steady StrollerSome walking, with rides between stops and places to rest
Pace: Willing WalkerLong periods of walking, climbing or hiking, with no place to sit and little rest or rides in transportation

Choosing Your Corfu Day

Paleokastritsa and Old Town are at the top of the Corfu wish list. Some tours do both, and some stay out in the countryside for a quieter day.

Want both Paleokastritsa and Old Town?

Choose from Options A to D. Option A is the easiest and has no museum. Option D lets the group set its own plan.

Want an easy day away from town?

Choose from Options E to H: tastings, beaches and coastal views, mostly from the comfort of the van.

Want a special food and wine day?

Option I is Kensington's private cooking class and five-course lunch at a family vineyard estate.

If the group splits for the day, that's perfectly OK. Everyone meets back on the ship.

How the options are grouped

Paleokastritsa and Old Town: Options A, B, C, D

The coast and the monastery, plus time in Corfu's Old Town.

Countryside and the Coast: Options E, F, G, H

Relaxed days on the scenic side of the island, with no Old Town.

A Higher-End Choice: Option I

A fully arranged private food and wine day from Kensington Tours.

Good to Know

Paleokastritsa Monastery

We'll ask each driver to go all the way up to the monastery so the walk in is short.

Sea cave boat rides

The boats are small, with a step down from the dock, and they only run in calm seas. The ride is an extra cost on most tours.

Old Town

We'll ask for drop-off at the Spianada and the Liston, which is flat, open and full of cafés. The lanes behind it are stone and uneven.

Riding together

For all six of us, we'll ask for a van or minibus rather than a car so no one is squeezed into a back seat.

Prices

Listed prices can change with group size. Final pricing for six is confirmed when Nov 4 is chosen on the booking page.
