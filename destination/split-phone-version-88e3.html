Split

Diocletian's Palace, Trogir & the Dalmatian Coast
Cruise Day 6 · Enchanted Princess

Split is Croatia's second city, built in and around the palace a Roman emperor, Diocletian, built here around 300 AD. People still live and shop inside its walls. Half an hour up the coast, Trogir is a small, flat island old town and one of the easiest places on this coast to explore on foot.

Towns or Waterfalls?

Split offers two very different days. One is a guided walk through the Roman palace and Trogir's old town. The other is a day at the Krka waterfalls, for those who enjoy a longer walk outdoors.

Want history and old towns?

Option A covers both Split and Trogir with a guide who walks with you. Option B is a shorter two-hour walk in Split, leaving time for lunch on the waterfront.

Want the waterfalls?

Peggy loved the look of the waterfall days. Options C and D visit Krka National Park, and Option E is Kensington's version with a guide, Trogir and a boat ride to the falls. The park walk has some steps, so go at your own pace and take the benches along the way.

If the group splits for the day, that's perfectly OK. Everyone meets back on the ship.

How the options are grouped

Guided Walks in the Old Towns: Options A, B

A private guide walks with you through Split's palace, and on Option A, through Trogir too.

Krka Waterfalls: Options C, D

The park's famous falls. See the main viewpoint with a short walk, or do the full loop of about 1.2 miles.

A Higher-End Choice: Option E

A fully arranged private day from Kensington Tours, with Trogir and the Krka waterfalls.

Good to Know

Walking in Split

The palace streets are flat, polished stone, which can be slippery when wet, so shoes with good grip are a good idea. The cellars have a few steps down and are easy to skip.

Trogir

Small and flat. It's about 30 minutes from Split and one of the easiest old towns on this coast.

Krka National Park

The loop is about 1.2 miles on boardwalks and gravel, with some steps. Park tickets are about €30 each, paid on the day.
