The smartest travel habits

  • Carry passports, medicine, valuables and one change of clothes onboard.
  • Photograph passports, tickets and baggage receipts.
  • Be ready 10-15 minutes before each transfer.
  • Keep cases light enough for trains and hotel changes.
  • Leave room to enjoy the moment instead of filling every hour.

Passports, borders & customs

Portugal and Spain are one Schengen journey

Passport

Carry the physical passport in a secure personal item, never in checked luggage. It should remain valid at least three months beyond the planned Schengen departure; six months is the safer travel standard.

ETIAS

No ETIAS action is currently required. Applications are not open. The official EU has not confirmed a firm launch date, despite increasing expectations of a 2027 start. Do not pay an unofficial website.2

EES

The EU Entry/Exit System is separate from ETIAS. It may record a facial image and fingerprints at the Lisbon border. This happens during passport processing and is not something Mike and Ann purchase.1

Portugal to Spain

The cruise call at Vega de Terrón, the flight from Lisbon to Bilbao and the entire Basque extension remain within the Schengen Area. Normally there is no routine passport-control booth between Portugal and Spain, but carry the passport on every flight and ship-directed excursion.

Leaving Europe

On November 11, Bilbao-Madrid is domestic Schengen travel. Schengen exit passport control occurs in Madrid before the Chicago flight. The 1 hour 35 minute connection is purposeful, so follow Connections / Conexiones signs immediately.

U.S. customs

Chicago is the first U.S. entry point. Complete immigration, retrieve checked luggage, clear customs and then recheck for Detroit unless CBP or airline staff specifically direct otherwise.5

Lisbon · October 24-28

Wine, music and golden light

SAT · OCT 24

Arrival and Cervejaria Ribadouro

Settle in, rest and walk about five minutes down Avenida da Liberdade for the 7:00 p.m. group dinner. Shellfish can be priced by weight, so confirm the price before ordering premium crustaceans.

What surprises visitors: Jet lag often arrives suddenly at dinner. Keep the first evening easy.

SUN · OCT 25

Private tuk-tuk and Mesa de Frades fado

Hotel pickup is 9:00 a.m. for a three-hour Lisbon overview. The tuk-tuk covers hilly districts and viewpoints with less walking. Take a taxi to Mesa de Frades for the 6:30 p.m., four-hour dinner and fado experience.

What it feels like: The tuk-tuk is lively and bumpy; fado is intimate and emotional. Conversation quiets once the singers begin.

MON · OCT 26

Sintra, Pena Palace and Tagus sunset

Private pickup is 9:00 a.m.; the timed Pena Palace entry is 11:00 a.m. The four-to-five-hour tour is followed by a 4:00 p.m. motorboat cruise from Doca de Santo Amaro.

Timing matters: Tell the Sintra guide about the boat before departure. Pena is steep and crowded, and traffic returning to Lisbon can be slow. Transportation to the dock is not included.

Bring: Stable shoes, a rain layer and a warm wind-resistant layer for the boat.

TUE · OCT 27

Private Setúbal wine country

Pickup is 9:30 a.m. for roughly six hours. José Maria da Fonseca is the core winery; other stops depend on availability. Seven to ten tastings are listed.

Confirm: Whether the full lunch option is included. Eat breakfast and carry water.

Rail & embarkation · October 28

A carefully connected morning

Sequence: 8:00 a.m. hotel pickup → Lisboa-Oriente → Alfa Pendular 182 at 10:09 a.m. → Vila Nova de Gaia at 12:43 p.m. → 1:15 p.m. transfer → A-ROSA ALVA, cabin D317.

At Lisboa-Oriente

Stay together until the platform is posted. Match carriage and seat numbers on the tickets. Keep one hand free for escalators and boarding.

On the train

First class offers reserved seating and more space. Larger cases go in designated luggage racks; keep passports, wallets, phones and medicine with you.

Gaia transfer

The train-to-transfer margin is 32 minutes. If the train is delayed, contact the transfer provider before arrival. Do not make an independent taxi decision until the provider has been reached.

Embarkation

The ship departs Porto at 3:00 p.m. Once onboard, confirm the safety briefing, dining rhythm, excursion-desk hours and the daily-program delivery method.

A-ROSA ALVA · October 28-November 4

The Douro cruise rhythm

Oct 29 · Régua and Pinhão

Morning sailing, a brief Régua call and Pinhão overnight. The tiled railway station and riverfront make a rewarding stroll.

Oct 30 · Pinhão to Barca d'Alva

Protect time for scenic daytime sailing. Barca d'Alva is peaceful and functions mainly as an excursion gateway.

Oct 31-Nov 1 · Vega de Terrón

The ship crosses into Spain and stays overnight. A Salamanca excursion means a substantial coach ride and uneven historic paving.

Nov 1-2 · Pinhão and Régua

The return reveals the valley from a new angle. There is no need to duplicate every wine tasting simply because one is offered.

Nov 2-3 · Leverinho and Porto

Porto is steep and cobbled. Consider a taxi-assisted or panoramic plan to save energy for the Spain extension.

Onboard

Keep a layer, sunglasses and camera near the lounge or sun deck. Locks and vineyard scenery are part of the experience; river operations can change timings.

Your cabin: D317, a two-bed outside Category D cabin with a drop-down panorama window. A-ROSA's Market Restaurant uses open seating and buffet service. Limited themed dinners may require signup and an extra charge.
Basque Spain · November 5-11

San Sebastián and Bilbao

CONFIRMED means the itinerary provides a booking or confirmation. PLANNED / RESERVE means it appears as a recommended plan and may still require a direct reservation.
THU · NOV 5

Lisbon to Bilbao, then pintxos in San Sebastián

CONFIRMED The hotel-to-Lisbon Airport transfer is scheduled for 6:00 a.m. TAP TP1062 leaves Lisbon at 8:50 a.m. and arrives Bilbao at 11:25 a.m. The transfer meets at 12:10 p.m. for Hotel Lasala Plaza. At 6:15 p.m., meet near the tobacco shop in front of City Hall for the three-hour food tour.

What it feels like: The food tour is a moving dinner through busy standing-room bars. Wear comfortable shoes and advise the operator of dietary restrictions in advance.

FRI · NOV 6

Basque Coast and Akelarre

CONFIRMED Hotel pickup is 9:00 a.m. for a five-to-eight-hour coastal day through Orio, Zarautz, Getaria and Zumaia, including a 50-minute Flysch boat trip. Dinner is reserved at Akelarre for 8:30 p.m.

Protect the reservation: Akelarre's listed cancellation/no-show charge is €150 per person within 48 hours. Allow time to return, rest and dress for dinner.

SAT · NOV 7

A restorative and very Basque day

PLANNED / RESERVE La Perla thalassotherapy at 10:00 a.m.; Old Town and Bretxa Market around 1:00 p.m.; La Concha promenade, Peine del Viento and Monte Igueldo in the afternoon; cider-house dinner at Petritegi at 8:00 p.m.

Weather plan: Reserve the spa and cider house. Check the funicular that morning; rain or wind can affect winter operations. If the coast is rough, enjoy Peine del Viento from a safe distance.

SUN · NOV 8

Transfer to Bilbao and the Guggenheim

CONFIRMED Hotel pickup is 10:00 a.m. for the transfer to Hotel Carlton Bilbao. PLANNED / RESERVE Guggenheim visit around 1:30 p.m. and Los Fueros dinner at 7:30 p.m.

Arrival flow: Ask Hotel Carlton to store the bags before the 3:00 p.m. check-in. Buy a timed Guggenheim ticket only after ensuring the transfer allows a comfortable arrival.

MON · NOV 9

Bizkaia mountain villages

CONFIRMED · 9:00 A.M. The private Duranguesado route includes Urkiola, Gorbeia, Otzarreta beech forest, Elorrio, Argineta and the Atxondo Valley. Lunch and snacks are not included. Ledesma Street is suggested for a casual evening graze.

TUE · NOV 10

Dragonstone, Mundaka and Guernica

CONFIRMED Hotel pickup is 9:00 a.m. for a six-hour tour of San Juan de Gaztelugatxe, Mundaka and Gernika. Lunch is not included. Farewell dinner at La Viña del Ensanche is planned for 7:30 p.m.

Walking reality: Gaztelugatxe is weather-dependent and requires moderate fitness. Stable shoes, water and a rain layer are essential; the viewpoint is still worthwhile if the full stair route is unsuitable or restricted.

Bilbao airport

Madrid · 1h 35m

IB430 arrives at 10:25 a.m.; IB341 leaves for Chicago at noon from Terminal 4S. Remain airside, follow Connections signs and complete Schengen exit passport control. Iberia operates the through itinerary under one record, PZ9F1.4

Packing & comfort

For cities, ship decks, coast and countryside

Clothing

  • Two pairs of supportive nonslip walking shoes
  • Light layers for changing autumn weather
  • Warm, packable rain jacket
  • Wind layer for boats and ship decks
  • Smart-casual outfit for Akelarre
  • Swimsuit for La Perla, if reserved

Essentials

  • Type C/F plug adapters
  • Portable charger in carry-on
  • Secure crossbody or compact day bag
  • Refillable water bottle
  • Motion-sickness remedy for coastal boat
  • Medication list with generic names

Walking reality

  • Lisbon and Porto are hilly and cobbled.
  • Pena Palace has steep slopes and crowds.
  • Pintxos tours involve standing and walking.
  • Coastal paths can be wet and windy.
  • Gaztelugatxe can be strenuous.

Dining rhythm

  • Pintxos are small bites, often eaten standing.
  • Choose euros at card terminals.
  • Confirm shellfish prices by weight.
  • Dietary restrictions should be shared early.
  • Protect cancellation deadlines for fine dining.
