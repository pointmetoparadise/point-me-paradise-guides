Wednesday 28 or Thursday 29 OctoberAthens — a day on the water

YOU LIKED THIS — NOT BOOKED

We would suggest the sailing lunch for either the 28th or the 29th — whichever suits you better once flights are settled. Friday is spoken for, so these are the two days it fits.

Sail & Taste the Greek Way

$1,380.60for all six of you — $230.10 each.
One welcome drink included; further wine approx. €22 a bottle.
What it is
A 42-foot sailboat out of Yacht Club Amfitheas in Paleo Faliro, private to just the six of you. You motor or sail up the coast, anchor somewhere sheltered, and eat a ten-course Greek lunch cooked on board by the skipper.
How long
About five hours, roughly three of them at sea
Once aboard
No walking at all. Cushioned deck seating, shade, and you settle in for the afternoon.
Worth knowing
Getting on is the part to think about — a step down from the dock onto a deck that moves, and the restroom is below deck down a short, steep ladder. If you would rather not be on a moving surface, say so and we will sort you out for that day.

Friday 30 OctoberAthens — Guards, Coastlines & Hidden Corners

YOU LIKED THIS — NOT BOOKED

You pause at Syntagma Square for the changing of the guard at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier — on the hour the Evzones step into position in traditional dress, in a slow and very precise ceremony. Then a private driver and English-speaking guide take you down the coast road to Cape Sounion and the Temple of Poseidon, through Glyfada, Vouliagmeni and Varkiza with the Saronic Gulf alongside.

It is a driving day with one proper stop at the end, and there is a level viewing area at the temple for anyone who would rather not walk up. This one suits everybody.

Saturday 31 OctoberAthens to Piraeus — boarding day

PLANNED

We will arrange a transfer to Piraeus for every one of you, wherever you are staying. Nobody has to sort their own way to the port.

Where two or more of you are at the same hotel and have the same boarding time, we can put you in one vehicle and share it — which is more pleasant and costs less each. Where the hotels or the times differ, you simply get your own transfer timed to your boarding slot.

To sort that out we will need to know which hotel you are in and the boarding time Princess has given you. Once we have both we can see who pairs up. The ship sails at 7:00 PM.

Sunday 1 NovemberSantorini — on hold

ON HOLD — ONE QUESTION FOR YOU

We are holding this day until we hear from each of you.

Santorini is a tender port. The ship anchors offshore and you come in by tender to the Old Port, which sits at the bottom of the cliff. The town is at the top. There are three ways up — a long flight of steps, a mule, or the cable car. The cable car is the only one of the three we would put in front of you.

What we know about the cable car

Season
We travel in low season, so queues should be shorter than the summer reports you may have read.
Winter timetable
It runs a reduced winter schedule — a morning block and an afternoon block rather than all day. For example 7:30 to 10:30, then 2:30 to 6:00, closed over the middle of the day.
When a ship is in
Hours are often extended or shifted to match the ship’s schedule. We are asking for the actual hours for 1 November in writing.
Fare
€10 one way per adult. €5 for children aged 6 to 12.
Weather
Strong November winds or storms can suspend the service temporarily.
The question. Would you be willing to ride the cable car up and down? It is a short ride in a small cabin, suspended over the cliff, with a queue at each end. Some people love it and some would rather not. Just tell us yes or no — both answers are completely fine, nobody needs a reason, and your answers decide how we plan the day.
