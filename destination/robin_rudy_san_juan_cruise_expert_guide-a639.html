Five habits that make this trip smoother

  • Keep passports, medicines, valuables and one change of clothes in the carry-on.
  • Photograph passports, baggage tags and excursion meeting instructions.
  • Be at excursion meeting points 10–15 minutes early.
  • Carry small cash for tips, taxis and local vendors.
  • Build in rest between port days; five consecutive islands is wonderfully busy.
Airports & layovers

Exactly what happens along the way

Austin AUS · Thursday, October 15

Your JetBlue journey begins at 7:15 a.m. on B6 2272 to Fort Lauderdale. Because wheelchair assistance was requested, arrive early enough that the assistance team can meet you without rushing security or boarding.

Before leaving the counter: Confirm both boarding passes and ask whether checked bags are tagged through to SJU.

Fort Lauderdale FLL · 1h 30m connection

You arrive at 11:00 a.m. and B6 1453 departs at 12:45 p.m. The itinerary notes in-between-flight assistance. This is a connection, not a sightseeing layover. Follow JetBlue connection signage and let the wheelchair attendant know your next gate immediately.

Reality: 90 minutes is workable, but do not linger for a full meal until you know the next gate.

San Juan SJU · arrival 3:23 p.m.

For U.S. citizens, Puerto Rico is a domestic arrival: no U.S. customs or international passport-control process at the airport. Collect any checked bags, meet your taxi and head to the Condado Palm Inn.

Easy first evening: Check in, eat near the hotel and keep sightseeing light. The next day is the real San Juan experience.

Return · Saturday, October 24

The ship returns to San Juan in the morning. JetBlue B6 1454 departs SJU at 2:37 p.m., arriving FLL at 5:20 p.m. Your connection is a generous 3h 43m; B6 2271 then leaves at 8:59 p.m. for Austin, arriving at 11:09 p.m.

Do not cut the airport transfer close. Disembarkation lines, baggage collection and traffic can vary.

Luggage & carry-on

Pack for flights, ship and five island days

JetBlue cabin limits

JetBlue currently allows one carry-on up to 22 x 14 x 9 inches and one personal item up to 17 x 13 x 8 inches. Checked bags are generally limited to 50 lb and 62 total linear inches; the exact fee or included allowance depends on the fare purchased.

Lisa note: Recheck the reservation’s exact checked-bag inclusion shortly before departure rather than assuming a bag is included.

Never put these in the checked suitcase

  • Passports and government ID
  • Prescription medicine
  • Royal Caribbean boarding documents
  • Phones, chargers and valuables
  • One change of clothes and swimwear
  • Bio-bay clothes you can get wet in

Embarkation-day bag

Checked luggage may not reach the cabin immediately. Keep anything needed for the first afternoon and evening with you: medications, swimsuit, sunscreen, glasses, electronics and dinner basics.

Mobility comfort

Several booked excursions involve walking, uneven ground, boats or open-air vehicles. Lightweight supportive shoes, a compact day bag and a reusable water bottle will matter more than dressy daytime footwear.

San Juan · before the cruise

What is truly worth your limited time

You have two nights in Condado, but Friday afternoon and evening are already committed to Laguna Grande. The smartest plan is to enjoy Old San Juan Friday morning and keep Saturday gentle before boarding.

[MUST DO] El Morro + Old San Juan

Allow: 2–3 hours. Go in the morning before the strongest heat. The broad lawn, Atlantic views, colorful streets and centuries-old fortifications are the San Juan experience that lives up to the photographs.

What surprises visitors: the hills and walking. Taxi or rideshare uphill, then explore mostly downhill if energy or mobility is a concern.

[FREE] Paseo de la Princesa + San Juan Gate

Allow: 45–75 minutes. This waterfront walk feels calmer than the shopping streets and gives you the scale of the old city walls. Late afternoon is especially pretty, but Friday morning also works as part of an Old San Juan loop.

[BOOKED] Laguna Grande bioluminescent kayak

Your pickup is 4:00 p.m. Friday at the DoubleTree by Hilton San Juan. The experience is about five hours with transportation. Expect to get wet from the waist down; bring a towel, change of clothes and insect repellent.

Important: participants must be able to swim. Your itinerary lists a 240-lb maximum per person. Brightness changes with moon, tides, rain and other natural conditions.

[FREE] Condado morning

Your hotel makes a sunrise beach walk easy. Condado is excellent for the first coffee and ocean air, but surf can be rough. Treat the beach as a walk-and-view stop unless conditions are clearly safe.

Royal Caribbean · Rhapsody of the Seas

Embarkation and life onboard

Saturday, October 17

The ship is scheduled to depart San Juan at 8:00 p.m.. Your hotel checkout is 11:00 a.m. Royal Caribbean currently lists its San Juan embarkation terminal as Pan American Pier 2, Calle Muelle.

Say the pier name. Do not tell a driver only “the cruise port.” Old San Juan has multiple piers; confirm Pan American Pier 2 in the Royal Caribbean app/documents on sailing day.

Travel documents

Royal Caribbean strongly recommends passports, and for San Juan-origin sailings that visit foreign islands they are the cleanest, safest form of identification. Keep original documents with you at terminal check-in — never in the suitcase handed to porters.

Good onboard choices

Rhapsody is a smaller, classic Royal Caribbean ship. Prioritize the Viking Crown Lounge for views, Schooner Bar for piano evenings, the Solarium for quieter adult time and one specialty dinner if you want an occasion meal.

Sea day · Friday, October 23

After five port days, resist the urge to schedule every hour. Sleep in, enjoy the Solarium, have a long lunch and let this day be recovery rather than another checklist.

Your port days

What each island should feel like

Oct 18 · St. Thomas

In port: 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. This is turquoise-water Caribbean with dramatic hills, overlooks and beach choices. If an excursion is already booked, use it as your anchor and leave shopping as the optional extra, not the main event.

Best surprise: how steep and green Charlotte Amalie looks from the water.

Oct 19 · Tortola

Your itinerary includes a local-style Road Town experience with Queen Elizabeth II Park, the J.R. O’Neal Botanic Gardens, local fish and fungi, an open-air trolley, Old Government House Museum, gelato and Craft’s Alive Village.

Feel: more culture and everyday island life than a beach-only tour. The excursion notes say it is not wheelchair accessible and not recommended for travelers with back problems.

Oct 20 · St. Maarten · [BOOKED]

9:00 a.m. “Both Sides One Perfect Day” — 4–5 hours. Stops include Cole Bay lookout, Topper’s Rhum, Maho Beach, Marigot and Orient Bay. Your guide meets outside the main port gate.

What to expect: a busy highlight reel with genuine variety. Maho is famous and fun, but it is crowded and noisy — enjoy the spectacle rather than expecting a relaxing beach.

Oct 21 · Antigua

Antigua is famous for beaches, but your itinerary also highlights the historic side of St. John’s and Nelson’s Dockyard. If your booked tuk-tuk experience is operating as listed, the Dockyard entry is included.

Watch the fine print: the tour notes indicate walking and an adults-only brewery component; anyone with walking difficulty or a mobility device should review suitability before sailing.

Oct 22 · Dominica · [BOOKED]

9:30 a.m. PH Whale Watch & Dolphin Cruise. Dominica is the “Nature Island,” and this is exactly the kind of experience that fits it: time on the water, dramatic volcanic coastline and the chance to spot sperm whales and dolphins.

Meeting: cruise-ship guests are met at the dock in Roseau. Wildlife is never guaranteed, so judge the day by the scenery and experience as well as sightings.

Oct 23 · At sea

You earned this one. Five islands in five days is a wonderful run, but it is physically full. Make the sea day restorative: breakfast without an alarm, pool or Solarium time, a show and packing before dinner rather than at midnight.

Packing

What will earn its place in the suitcase

Daytime essentials

  • 2 swimsuits so one can dry
  • Lightweight quick-dry clothing
  • Supportive walking shoes
  • Water shoes or secure sandals
  • Hat and reef-friendly sunscreen
  • Compact rain layer
  • Reusable water bottle

Excursion kit

  • Small crossbody or daypack
  • Waterproof phone pouch
  • Insect repellent for bio bay
  • Small bills for tips/vendors
  • Copy/photo of excursion voucher
  • Ship card and required ID

Evenings onboard

Rhapsody evenings range from casual to smart casual, with occasional dressier events. Bring one outfit that feels special, but do not let formalwear take over the suitcase.

Medication & comfort

Bring more prescription medication than the exact trip length, plus motion-sickness support if boats can bother you. Keep all prescription medicine in your personal item.
