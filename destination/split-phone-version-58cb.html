Split

Diocletian's Palace, Trogir & the Dalmatian Coast
Cruise Day 6 · Enchanted Princess

Split is Croatia's second city, built in and around the palace a Roman emperor, Diocletian, built here around 300 AD. People still live and shop inside its walls. Half an hour up the coast, Trogir is a small, flat island old town and one of the easiest places on this coast to explore on foot.

Every tour below is private, just for our group.

Towns or Waterfalls?

Split offers two very different days. One is a guided walk through a Roman emperor's palace and Trogir's little island old town. The other is a trip out to the Krka waterfalls, one of Croatia's most beautiful natural sights.

Want history and old towns?

Option A covers both Split and Trogir with a guide who walks with you at your pace. Option D adds the Roman ruins of Salona and Klis Fortress for a fuller day.

Want the waterfalls?

Peggy loved the look of the waterfall days. Options B, C, E, F and G all visit Krka National Park. Option E is the shortest and simplest, and Option F adds a river cruise and a wine tasting. You can see the main falls with a short walk and skip the stairs on the full loop.

Want a fully arranged day?

Option H is Kensington's private day with a guide, Trogir, and a boat ride right up to the falls.

If the group splits for the day, that's perfectly OK. Everyone meets back on the ship.

Good to Know

Our time in port

The ship is in Split from about 10 AM to 7 PM. We'll ask each company for a start time around 10:30 so everyone is back with plenty of time to spare. The longer days may be shortened a little to fit.

Walking in Split

The palace streets are flat, polished stone, which can be slippery when wet, so shoes with good grip are a good idea.

Trogir

Small and flat. It's about 30 minutes from Split and one of the easiest old towns on this coast.

Krka National Park

The main falls are a short, easy walk. The full loop is mostly boardwalk and paved paths but has a fair number of stairs, so it's fine to see the highlights and relax on a bench. Park tickets are usually paid on the day, and they cost less in November.
