Katakolon

Olympia & the Peloponnese Countryside
Cruise Day 4 · Enchanted Princess

Katakolon is a small harbor village on Greece's western coast. It is the doorway to Ancient Olympia, where the first Olympic Games were held, and to a quiet countryside of olive groves, vineyards and family farms.

Every tour below is private, just for our group, with its own driver. Olympia is about a 35 to 40 minute drive from the ship.

One Port, Two Kinds of Day

Lisa would love to see Ancient Olympia and its Archaeological Museum. Others in the group would rather skip museums and have an easy day. Both are completely doable, and nobody has to give up her day for someone else.

Stay together on one tour

Book Option A or B. Everyone rides together, and at Olympia, Lisa and anyone who wants to join her head into the site and museum while the others relax at a café in Olympia village. Then everyone is back together for the tastings.

Go separate ways for the day

Most of the group takes the Olive Oil Tour & Lunch (Option F), while Lisa and anyone who joins her book a single van on Option C for up to four. We can add a licensed guide inside the museum if she'd like one. Then we all meet back on the ship and swap stories.

Parting ways at a port is perfectly OK. It's often the best way to make sure everyone has the day she wants.

Good to Know

Walking at Olympia

The ancient site is about an hour on gravel and packed dirt, with uneven stones and patchy shade. The museum is flat and air-conditioned, with places to sit. Comfortable closed shoes and a hat are a good idea.

Guides inside the site

These are driver tours. The driver talks about the area on the way and drops you at the entrance, but only a licensed guide may lead inside the site and museum. We can arrange one for an extra charge.

Entry fees

The Olympia site and museum ticket is about €20 each, paid on the day. Tasting and lunch fees are listed on each tour page.

Riding together

Options A, B, D and E have one flat price that covers all six in one vehicle. Option C is priced per van of up to four, so we'll confirm they send one larger van for everyone.
