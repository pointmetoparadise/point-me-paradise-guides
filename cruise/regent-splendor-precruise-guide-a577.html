How to Book Your Shore Excursions — and More

Your window is open now — here is exactly what to click
Sailing 11 December 2026 Seven Seas Splendor Serenity Suite
Before You Begin

One task matters right now

Everything else on this page can wait its turn

Your shore excursions are included in your fare — every port, no per-tour charge, no upsell waiting at the gangway. What is not unlimited is availability. Because they cost nothing extra, every guest books them, and the strongest tours in each port commit quickly.

Your booking window opened on 14 June 2026 and closes 4 December 2026. It is open as you read this. The walkthrough below takes about an hour and is the single most useful hour you can spend on this voyage.

We don't book hotels. We build trips. Custom guides, trip app, real human when something goes sideways. The work you'd hate doing — already done.
Step by Step

Booking your excursions at rssc.com

Nine steps, start to finish

Have these in front of you first

Two minutes of setup saves twenty of hunting
  • Your Regent booking number — on your confirmation, or email me and I'll send it
  • Each guest's name exactly as it appears on the reservation
  • Your sail date: 11 December 2026
  • A credit card — needed only if you select a Regent Choice tour (explained in Step 5)
  • Your port times for each day — visible in the booking tool, but useful to note as you go
1

Create or sign in to My Account

Go to rssc.com and choose My Account at the top right of the page. If you already have a login, sign in and skip to Step 2.

If this is your first time, choose Register or Create an Account. Regent will ask for your booking number, your last name as it appears on the reservation, and your sail date. Use the same email address your booking is under so the two link automatically.

Do this today, not on the morning you plan to book. Account setup occasionally needs a verification email or a call to Regent, and that is a frustrating thing to discover with a tour list open in another tab.

2

Open your voyage

Once signed in, find the Booked Cruises tab. Depending on your view, this may be labeled Manage This Booking instead — both lead to the same place.

Confirm you are looking at the 11 December 2026 Seven Seas Splendor voyage before you go further.

3

Click Customize Shore Excursions

Beside your voyage you will see Customize Shore Excursions. This opens the booking tool, which is organized by day rather than by tour type.

You will land on a day-by-day list of your itinerary. Sea days show no excursions; port days show every tour available in that port.

4

Check the port times before you choose anything

Each port day displays your arrival and departure times. Read those first. A day in port from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. supports a very different plan than one from 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m., and a tender port costs you time on both ends of the day getting ashore and back.

Work one day at a time, top to bottom. Trying to browse the whole catalog at once is how people end up double-booked.

5

Open each tour and read the detail before adding it

Click into a tour rather than adding from the list view. The detail page gives you the duration, the activity level, whether a meal is included, and any minimum or maximum number of participants.

Watch for the Regent Choice label. The vast majority of the catalog is included in your fare at no cost. A smaller set of premium experiences is marked Regent Choice and carries a supplementary charge, payable by major credit card at the moment you confirm. Everything unlabeled is included.

6

Select every traveling guest — on every tour

When you add a tour, the system asks which guests are going. It does not carry your answer forward to the next tour.

This is the single most common mistake guests make: booking a tour for one guest and not the other, then discovering it at the pier. Check the passenger selection on every excursion before you move on. It takes three seconds each and it is far easier to fix now than on the morning of.

7

Review your cart day by day before checkout

Go back through your selections in order and confirm nothing overlaps. If you have stacked two excursions in the same port, the system requires a 75-minute gap between them — and that spacing is worth having anyway as a buffer if the first tour returns late.

Confirm each tour ends comfortably before your all-aboard time. Regent holds the ship for its own excursions, but you do not want to be testing that on a short port call.

8

Complete checkout

Proceed to checkout even though most or all of your tours show a zero balance — selections are not held until you finish this step. If you have chosen any Regent Choice tours, enter your card here.

You will receive a confirmation email listing every tour and every guest. Read it against your cart. If a guest is missing from a tour, this is where you will catch it.

9

Save your confirmation — and know how to change things later

Keep the confirmation email somewhere you can find it — it is your record of who is booked on what.

You can cancel or modify online through 4 December 2026, seven days before you sail. After that date the online tool closes and changes are made at the Destination Services desk aboard, subject to what is still available.

Working the System

Three things that separate a good excursion list from a lucky one

What experienced Regent guests do differently

Reserve generously at the opening

The included tours cost you nothing to hold. It is far easier to release one later than to claim a sold-out seat in November. If you are torn between two tours in a port, take both now and decide closer in.

Take the wait list — it moves

Wait lists clear more often than guests expect. Regent works them roughly once a week, in the order requests were received, asking local operators to add capacity — another guide, another coach. Space depends on what each supplier can source for your specific sail date, so nothing is guaranteed, but a great many guests clear.

Know why your window is 180 days

Regent opens shore excursions in tiers. Concierge Suites and above, and Seven Seas Society members at Silver level and above, book at 210 days. The Serenity Suite sits just below Concierge in Regent's category order, and since this is your first Regent sailing you have not yet built Society status — so your window is the 180-day one.

Worth knowing for next time

This voyage earns you Society nights. On a future Regent cruise that status, or a Concierge Suite, moves you a full month earlier in line. It is a real advantage in ports where the signature tour holds forty seats.

Dining, Classes and Spa

Your other reservations

Three windows, three different booking methods

Specialty dining

Opens 8:00 p.m. Eastern, 27 September 2026

Sign in to My Account and open the Dining Reservations link under your Booked Cruises. Note the hour — it is an evening opening rather than a midnight one, and it is precise. Be logged in and waiting a few minutes beforehand and you will get the nights you want.

  • Three restaurants take reservations: Chartreuse (French, Deck 10 aft), Prime 7 (steakhouse, Deck 10 aft) and Pacific Rim (Pan-Asian, Deck 5).
  • You may pre-book one dinner in each before you sail — three reservations total.
  • Additional reservations are arranged onboard with the Maître d', subject to space.
  • Compass Rose and Sette Mari at La Veranda never require a reservation. Walk in whenever you like.
  • Chartreuse also serves lunch on sea days and alternate port days — a quieter way to try it if the dinner nights don't work out.

Culinary Arts Kitchen

Opens 13 August 2026

Classes open through the Culinary Arts Kitchen link under your Booked Cruises. Class sizes are small by design and the sea-day sessions go first, so put a reminder on that date.

Serene Spa and Wellness

Reserve between 12 October and 4 December 2026

The spa is one of the few things aboard that is not included in your fare. Your fitness center, classes, sauna and relaxation lounge come with the voyage. Treatments do not. Massages, facials, body wraps, nail and hair services are all charged to your onboard account at destination spa pricing — plan for them the way you would a treatment at a fine resort.

Call 1.877.718.4244 or email SereneSpa@rssc.com. Email is the easier route if you would like to see the treatment menu and current pricing before you commit.

A note on timing

Shipboard credit cannot be applied to spa services reserved in advance through My Account. If any credit is due to post to your booking before you sail, book your treatments at the spa once you are aboard rather than prepaying online, so the credit is available to cover them. Sea days fill first, so stop by on embarkation day.

Documents and Dress

What arrives when, and what to pack

The last of the housekeeping

Celebrating something

If a birthday, anniversary or holiday celebration falls during your voyage, note it on your Guest Registration Form in My Account. Regent does this beautifully when they know in advance, and it costs nothing to tell them. Let me know as well and I will follow up from my side.

Your documents

Once final payment is received and your guest registration is complete, cruise documents are emailed to you. A welcome letter with luggage tags follows by traditional mail around 27 October, so please confirm that the mailing address on your reservation is current. Your final cruise vacation summary arrives by email around 20 November.

Dress code aboard

Daytime, until 6:00 p.m.

  • Resort-style clothing throughout, including shorts, warm-up suits, jeans and sneakers.
  • Bare feet belong on the Pool Deck only. Swimwear is fine at the Pool Bar and Grill, but not in any indoor venue.

Evening, after 6:00 p.m. — Elegant Casual

  • For women: skirts, slacks or refined denim with a blouse or sweater, or a pantsuit or dress.
  • For men: slacks or refined denim with a collared shirt. Jackets are optional.
  • Denim should be neither torn nor distressed. Dress sneakers are permitted when clean and in a neutral or classic color.
  • You are welcome to dress more formally if you enjoy it. Gowns, cocktail dresses, dinner jackets and dark suits all look at home here.
  • Not appropriate after 6:00 p.m. anywhere public: t-shirts, baseball caps, shorts, athletic shoes, flip-flops and bathrobes.

The final evening — Relaxed Casual

  • On the night before disembarkation the code relaxes entirely, since most guests are packing early for morning flights. Wear what is comfortable.
